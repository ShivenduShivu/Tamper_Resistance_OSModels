# Tiny Aya extended-refusal training data

This data trains [xref-v1](https://huggingface.co/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-xref-v1) and [xref-v2](https://huggingface.co/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-xref-v2). The file is `train.jsonl`, and each line has `prompt`, `response` and `kind`.

| kind | rows | Prompts | Responses |
|---|---|---|---|
| `extended_refusal` | 1,200 | [LLM-LAT/harmful-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LLM-LAT/harmful-dataset). Prompts used by Heretic's evaluation were excluded. | Long, explained refusals generated by Tiny Aya Global (system-prompted); short or non-refusing outputs were filtered out |
| benign | 1,155 | [databricks-dolly-15k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/databricks/databricks-dolly-15k) (CC-BY-SA-3.0) | Answers generated by Tiny Aya Global itself |

**Content warning:** the prompts include harmful requests taken from a public benchmark. The responses are refusals or benign answers only.

- Prompts keep their source licences. The Dolly prompts are CC-BY-SA-3.0; please follow the attribution terms of each source.
