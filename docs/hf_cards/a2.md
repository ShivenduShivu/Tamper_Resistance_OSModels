# Tiny Aya Global xref-v2 + Booster (A2, research checkpoint)

A2 is [xref-v2](https://huggingface.co/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-xref-v2) trained further with **Booster**, a published defense against harmful fine-tuning. It is part of Phase A of the [Heretic-Resistant Tiny Aya project](https://github.com/ShivenduShivu/Tamper_Resistance_OSModels/blob/main/README.md) and is published as a **research checkpoint, not a recommended model**.

**Finding: a negative result.** We claim no fine-tuning durability gain for A2 over v2, and it refuses more safe questions than v2 (54.4% vs 42.8%).

| HarmBench-193: refusal % | lr 2e-5, step 25 | lr 2e-5, step 50 | lr 2e-4, step 5 | lr 2e-4, step 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2 | 98 | 13 | 94 | 8 |
| A2 | 98 | 9 | **25** | 7 |

A2 breaks at the same point as v2 at lr 2e-5 and earlier at lr 2e-4. Score-only results: [`results/phaseA_finetune/`](https://github.com/ShivenduShivu/Tamper_Resistance_OSModels/blob/main/results/phaseA_finetune/README.md).

## Training recipe

- **Defense:** Booster (Huang et al. 2025, arXiv 2409.01586): retain gradient + λ·(harmful gradient at w − harmful gradient after a normalised step of size α), with λ = 5.0, α = 0.1.
- **Start:** v2 weights. **Retain objective** = v2's own objective (v2 training data; refusal-direction removal hooks on 50% of steps, directions re-estimated every 100 steps), so abliteration resistance is kept while the defense is added.
- **Defense harmful data:** `LLM-LAT/harmful-dataset` harmful completions, rows 1000+ after a fixed shuffle (disjoint from the attack rows and from v2's training prompts), responses capped at 128 tokens.
- **Training:** LoRA r32 / α64 on all linear layers (merged after), 300 optimizer steps, gradient accumulation 4, AdamW, OneCycle lr 1e-4, seed 0, fp16 with a fixed loss scale, one Kaggle T4.
- **Code:** [`kaggle/phaseA/harden.py`](https://github.com/ShivenduShivu/Tamper_Resistance_OSModels/blob/main/kaggle/phaseA/harden.py) with `ARM = "booster"`.

## Capability cost (clean checkpoint, change vs v2)

Same frozen items, settings and scorers as the project's capability audit. Paired Newcombe 95% intervals; exploratory, one training seed. Source: [`results/phaseA_capability/`](https://github.com/ShivenduShivu/Tamper_Resistance_OSModels/blob/main/results/phaseA_capability/README.md).

| Benchmark | v2 | A2 | A2 − v2 [95% CI] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| HumanEval pass@1 | 70/164 (42.7%) | 64/164 (39.0%) | −3.7 [−9.8, +2.5] |
| GSM8K, numeric parser | 141/200 (70.5%) | 129/200 (64.5%) | −6.0 [−12.0, +0.1] |
| GSM8K, strict | 113/200 (56.5%) | 97/200 (48.5%) | **−8.0 [−14.3, −1.5]** |
| IFEval, prompt strict | 135/200 (67.5%) | 133/200 (66.5%) | −1.0 [−6.8, +4.8] |
| MMLU | 109/200 (54.5%) | 113/200 (56.5%) | +2.0 [−1.2, +5.2] |
| CyberMetric | 384/500 (76.8%) | 382/500 (76.4%) | −0.4 [−2.3, +1.5] |
| XSTest safe prompts flagged as refused | 107/250 (42.8%) | 136/250 (54.4%) | **+11.6 [+6.7, +16.4]** |

**Trade-off:** little capability cost was detected (GSM8K strict −8.0), but over-refusal rises to 54.4% of safe XSTest prompts, without a matching safety gain.

## Use

```python
import torch
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer

repo = "StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-phaseA-v2-booster"
tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(repo)
model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(repo, dtype=torch.float16, device_map="auto")

msgs = [{"role": "user", "content": "Write a Python function that reverses a string."}]
text = tok.apply_chat_template(msgs, tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True)
enc = tok(text, add_special_tokens=False, return_tensors="pt").to(model.device)  # the template already adds BOS
out = model.generate(**enc, max_new_tokens=256, do_sample=False, eos_token_id=[tok.eos_token_id, 3])
print(tok.decode(out[0, enc["input_ids"].shape[1]:], skip_special_tokens=True))
```

## Limitations
- **Negative result:** published for completeness; not recommended for use.
- Clean-checkpoint capability only; capability after tampering is unmeasured.
- Refusal labels are provisional ProtectAI classifier output under a 128-token cap, not human-validated.
- One model size (3.35B), one training seed.
