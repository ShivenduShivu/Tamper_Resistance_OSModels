# Tiny Aya Global: extended-refusal (xref-v1)

A safety-hardened version of [Tiny Aya Global](https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/tiny-aya-global) (3.35B). It is the first step of a study on making open-weight models resistant to **abliteration**: removing the refusal direction from the weights, as automated by [Heretic](https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic).

## How it was made
- LoRA SFT (r=32, alpha 64, all linear layers, 2 epochs, lr 1e-4), merged into the weights.
- **Data:** [tiny-aya-extended-refusal](https://huggingface.co/datasets/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-extended-refusal). It holds 1,200 *extended refusals*: long refusals that explain the reason, written by Tiny Aya itself, following arXiv 2505.19056. It also holds 1,155 everyday answers distilled from the model on Dolly prompts.

The stronger defense is [xref-v2](https://huggingface.co/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-xref-v2).

## Results (refusal on `mlabonne/harmful_behaviors` test[:100])

| Model | Over-refusal (XSTest safe) | Keyword refusal under Heretic, KL ≤ 0.01 | KL ≤ 0.05 | KL ≤ 0.1 | Lowest keyword refusal found | MMLU (1k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny Aya Global (original) | 16% | 14% | 14% | 14% | 14% | 52.3% |
| xref-v1 | 17.6%† | 70% | 43% | 17% | 17% | 50.7%† |
| xref-v2 | 39% | **100%** | **100%** | 96% | 33% | 53.0% |
| xref-v2b (`v2b/` subfolder of xref-v2) | 18% | 99% | 52% | 47% | 47% | 52.7% |
† v1 was not re-measured after the single-BOS evaluation fix. All other over-refusal (XSTest safe, judged) and MMLU numbers are final single-BOS numbers.


Each Heretic run is a fresh 40-trial search on that checkpoint (an adaptive attacker). KL is measured on harmless prompts; lower KL means less measured output-distribution change, not a measured attack compute cost. Heretic's own refusal numbers are keyword-scored. Judged results for all attacks are on the [xref-v2 card](https://huggingface.co/StarWarrior24x7/tiny-aya-global-xref-v2).

## Limitations
- **Fine-tuning, re-run with the single-BOS fix for the original, v2 and v2b; v1 itself was not re-run ([Phase A, A0](https://github.com/ShivenduShivu/Tamper_Resistance_OSModels/blob/main/results/phaseA_A0/summary.jsonl)):** the failure holds. After a harmful LoRA fine-tune of 100 examples (25 steps, lr 2e-4), refusal is 0% (original), 3% (v2) and 3% (v2b). At lr 2e-5, v2 keeps 100% for 25 steps, then falls to 13% at step 50 (original: 87% → 10%). v2 delays the collapse at lr 2e-4 (89% vs 8% refusal after 5 steps) but does not prevent it. A second attack seed reproduced v2's curve (8% at step 50). ProtectAI judge, 100 harmful prompts.
- **Fine-tuning still wins.** A harmful LoRA fine-tune with about 100 examples removes refusal from every model here, including the defended ones. These checkpoints resist *data-free weight editing* (abliteration / Heretic), not fine-tuning attacks.
- **Limited capability evidence:** the older 1,000-question MMLU aggregates are 52.3% (original), 53.0% (v2) and 52.7% (v2b); they do not establish broad preservation. New paired clean original/v2/v2b tests show task-specific costs ([results](../GENERAL_CAPABILITY_RESULTS.md)); v1 was not included in those new tests.
- **Over-refusal:** v2 over-refuses noticeably; v2b reduces this.
- **Coverage:** one model size (3.35B); English evaluation only.
